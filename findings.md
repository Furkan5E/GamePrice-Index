## The same game on different stores

670 games were matched on at least two storefronts by normalised title. Regular prices are compared, so a temporary sale on one store does not count as that store being cheaper.

| Storefront | Shared games | Median price (£) | Median price elsewhere (£) | Average difference (%) | Cheapest or tied (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nintendo | 336 | 24.99 | 24.99 | 3.5 | 53.6 |
| PlayStation Store | 497 | 24.99 | 25.99 | -0.6 | 74.8 |
| Steam | 394 | 26.62 | 25.83 | 0.7 | 65.5 |
| Xbox | 422 | 27.11 | 28.49 | 1.3 | 59.7 |

- On shared games **PlayStation Store** averages -0.6% against the other stores and **Nintendo** +3.5%.
- Most multi-platform games cost the same everywhere; the gaps come from a minority of titles.

## How to read this

- Each storefront contributes its most popular games (1,000 by default), not its whole catalogue.
- Prices are UK prices in pounds, as listed on the day of the scrape.
- Title matching is deliberately strict: sequels, remasters and special editions count as different games.
