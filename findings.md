- Discounts run deepest on **Nintendo**, where the typical sale takes 70% off.

## The same game on different stores

685 games were matched on at least two storefronts by normalised title. Regular prices are compared, so a temporary sale on one store does not count as that store being cheaper.

| Storefront | Shared games | Median price (£) | Median price elsewhere (£) | Average difference (%) | Cheapest or tied (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nintendo | 331 | 26.99 | 26.86 | 6.1 | 52.3 |
| PlayStation Store | 505 | 32.99 | 32.99 | -0.2 | 74.5 |
| Steam | 414 | 34.99 | 34.24 | 0.6 | 68.8 |
| Xbox | 420 | 32.99 | 32.99 | 1.6 | 63.3 |

- On shared games **PlayStation Store** averages -0.2% against the other stores and **Nintendo** +6.1%.
- Most multi-platform games cost the same everywhere; the gaps come from a minority of titles.

## How to read this

- Each storefront contributes its most popular games (1,000 by default), not its whole catalogue.
- Prices are UK prices in pounds, as listed on the day of the scrape.
- Title matching is deliberately strict: sequels, remasters and special editions count as different games.
